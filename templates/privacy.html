Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects when you use our personal expense tracking application ("Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect basic profile information such as your name, email address, and password (stored securely, never in plain text). As you use the Service, we collect the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

We may also automatically collect limited technical information, such as browser type and access times, to help us maintain and improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to provide the core functionality of Spendly: authenticating your account, storing and displaying your expenses, generating summaries and category breakdowns, and improving the overall reliability of the Service. We do not use your financial data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our application database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time, after which your data will be removed except where retention is required for legal or operational reasons.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or loss, though no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or rent your personal or financial data to third parties. We may rely on trusted third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting or email delivery services) to operate the Service, and those providers only process data as necessary to perform their function on our behalf.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided within the Service, and we will be happy to assist.
